The digital experience relies entirely on cinematic storytelling, scroll-linked interactions, and advanced micro-animations to present a seamless narrative journey rather than a traditional partitioned website1. The primary objective of this document is to serve as the definitive single source of truth for faithfully reproducing the entire user experience. This encompasses the overarching visual language, spatial layout, precise motion choreography, and discrete component architecture required to reconstruct the interface using placeholder assets. Recognized by international design bodies such as Awwwards with perfect scores in design, usability, creativity, and content from top industry peers, the portfolio represents a masterclass in modern digital presentation2.

## **Global Motion Architecture and Animation Choreography**

The portfolio is defined by its sophisticated motion design, transforming static elements into a living, responsive environment that reacts to user input and scroll velocity1. Every interaction, transition, and scroll event is governed by a unified set of physical rules and easing curves, ensuring that the experience feels organic and cohesive rather than chaotic. The motion design is not treated as decoration, but as an intentional communicative layer that guides the user's spatial understanding of the digital environment.  
To maintain absolute consistency across the SPA, all animations adhere to specific bezier curves. User interface micro-interactions, such as hover states and button clicks, utilize a snappy easing curve estimated at cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1). This curve creates a rapid initial acceleration followed by a smooth, lingering deceleration over a duration of 0.3 to 0.4 seconds, mimicking the physical friction of real-world objects. For larger narrative movements, such as section entrances and scroll reveals, a highly dramatic exponential ease-out curve, estimated at cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), is employed. This allows massive typographic elements to quickly reach eighty percent of their destination before slowly and elegantly settling into their final state over 0.8 to 1.2 seconds. Cinematic parallax effects and scrubbed animations are bound directly to the user's scroll velocity, employing a linear easing smoothed by a mathematical damping factor to eliminate any jitter caused by uneven scrolling hardware.  
The SPA requires a meticulously choreographed pre-loader sequence to guarantee that all Document Object Model elements, custom web fonts, and high-resolution placeholder assets are completely loaded before the layout calculations execute. The initialization state covers the entire viewport with a solid global background overlay, presenting a minimal loading indicator perfectly centered on the screen. Once the background assets confirm a ready state, the loading indicator translates upward along the vertical axis by approximately 20 pixels and smoothly transitions its opacity to zero. The solid background overlay then acts as a cinematic curtain, sliding vertically out of the viewport over a 1.2-second duration using an exponential ease-out curve. As this overlay reveals the foundational Hero section beneath, the internal textual and visual elements of the Hero section execute their entrance choreography with a staggered delay of 0.1 seconds to prevent the user from being visually overwhelmed.  
Scroll progress serves as the primary engine driving the narrative timeline. The design requirements dictate the use of advanced intersection observation mechanics for all section entrances. Elements remain entirely hidden from the render tree, possessing zero opacity and a positive vertical translation offset, until their bounding box crosses a precise intersection threshold, typically calculated at fifteen percent from the bottom of the viewport. Certain narrative sections are required to lock into place within the viewport. This pinning behavior allows a container to remain stationary while its internal content continues to scroll, creating a multi-layered depth effect that traps the user's attention on a specific storyline before releasing them down the page. Additionally, velocity-based distortions apply subtle skew or scale transformations to imagery based entirely on the speed of the user's scroll wheel. Rapid scrolling slightly stretches the bounding boxes of images, which then elasticize back to their natural state when the scrolling ceases.  
The traditional operating system cursor is completely overridden and replaced by a custom programmatic element to heighten the immersive, interactive feel of the portfolio. The default state of this custom cursor consists of a tiny, 8-pixel circular dot matching the primary text color, equipped with a difference blend mode to ensure absolute visibility over any underlying background shade. This primary dot is tracked by a larger, 32-pixel circular aura ring that follows the precise coordinates of the primary dot with a slight mathematical delay. This delay relies on an interpolation damping factor to create an elastic, trailing physical effect. When the user's cursor approaches an interactive element, such as a primary call-to-action button or a navigation link, the outer ring snaps magnetically to the bounding box of the target element. The ring scales dynamically to encompass the target, while the element itself slightly translates toward the center of the cursor's current coordinates, creating a palpable sense of physical magnetism and gravity.

The left hemisphere contains the primary brand identifier, a distinct logo mark rendering the text "NESH®". The central column houses the primary navigation links, arranged horizontally. These links include destinations such as "Home", "About me", "projects", "What you get", "Services", "clients", and "Faq"1. The right hemisphere provides interactive conversion tools, specifically displaying a direct email address with an integrated "Copy to clipboard" function, juxtaposed next to a prominent "Book a Call" call-to-action button1. The typography for the navigation links strictly adheres to the caption metadata typographic style to remain unobtrusive.  
The motion behavior of the header is highly responsive to user intent. Upon the completion of the initial loading sequence, the entire header drops down from above the top edge of the viewport, translating into its zero-axis position. As the user scrolls downward past the initial hero section, the header dynamically transitions into a glassmorphic state. This transition injects a dark background color at seventy percent opacity paired with a heavy background blur filter, ensuring legibility against complex scrolling imagery. To maximize the reading area on smaller screens, a velocity-sensitive smart-hide mechanic is required. A rapid downward scroll triggers the header to translate vertically out of view, while any upward scrolling instantly translates it back into the viewport. Hovering over any navigation link triggers a rapid micro-interaction where an underline scales from zero to one hundred percent width, originating strictly from the left origin point. ## **Responsive and Accessibility Behaviors**

The single-page application must degrade and adapt gracefully across a wide range of devices without ever losing its premium, cinematic feel.  
Responsive adaptation priorities require strict breakpoint architecture. On large desktop environments exceeding 1440 pixels, the maximum container width locks, and fluid typography scales hit their maximum clamped value. On standard tablets ranging from 768 to 1023 pixels, the twelve-column grid collapses down into an eight-column structure. Massive hero typography reduces its scalar values, and multi-column layouts, such as the three-column pricing services, collapse into a two-column grid or a vertical stack. On mobile devices below 767 pixels, the grid collapses entirely into a four-column structure. All multi-column layouts stack vertically. Complex scroll-pinning mechanisms, specifically the locking functionality of the Timeline section, must be disabled entirely on mobile devices to prevent severe jank and viewport height bugs on touch interfaces. Timeline items simply stack vertically and reveal via basic opacity triggers as the user scrolls. Furthermore, the complex magnetic cursor interactions must be stripped entirely from the document on touch devices.  
While the experience is intensely motion-driven, the design requirements mandate adherence to fundamental accessibility protocols. All foreground text and background color combinations must mathematically meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA standards, ensuring a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1. Interactive elements must retain visible focus rings for users relying on keyboard navigation; a custom focus ring utilizing the primary accent color must manifest when a user utilizes the Tab key. Crucially, the system must listen for the operating system's reduced motion preferences. A global media query must automatically disable all scroll-linked parallax shifts, infinite floating math, and massive entrance staggers, rendering elements at full opacity in their final resting positions for any user with motion sensitivities.
